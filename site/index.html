01

Verhaal

In development Main project

2026 · Consumer claims recovery · The Netherlands

A platform that helps consumers recover what companies owe them, from the notice of default to the payment. A deterministic workflow engine owns the state, the statutory deadlines and the money; AI drafts the letters and answers the company at fixed points in the process, and a human reviews what leaves. Behind it: a consumer app, an admin cockpit with a review queue, and a go-live checklist that is as much legal as technical.

  • PostgreSQL
  • Claude API
  • Consumer law

2026 · Compliance automation · Bencis Capital Partners

Brief History

The path so far

I study the law where it meets finance. During my bachelor’s in Amsterdam it became clear that I did not want to be a classic litigator: the files that drew me in were about structuring M&A deals, negotiating terms and setting up fund structures. Since then, everything I have taken on — the internships, the treasurer’s seat, a podcast I co-founded and sold — has pointed the same way: towards private equity.
